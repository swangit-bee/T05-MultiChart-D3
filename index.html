TV Energy Consumption & Power Prices

Data visualisation exercise (COS30045)

This interactive dashboard explores the relationship between television energy consumption patterns and electricity spot prices in Australia. As consumer electronics continue to evolve, understanding the energy efficiency of different screen technologies and their long-term cost implications becomes increasingly important for both consumers and policymakers.

The visualizations below analyze data from various TV models and Australian electricity markets, providing insights into energy consumption trends across different screen technologies and sizes, as well as historical power pricing patterns from 1998 to 2024.

Scatter Plot: Energy Consumption vs Star Rating

This scatter plot shows the relationship between star ratings and energy consumption across all TV models. Each point represents a TV, colored by screen technology (LCD, LED, OLED). Higher star ratings generally indicate better energy efficiency.

Donut Chart: Energy Consumption by Screen Technology

This donut chart compares the average energy consumption across different screen technologies for all TV sizes combined. The visualization helps identify which technologies are most energy-efficient overall.

Bar Chart: 55-inch TV Energy Consumption

This bar chart focuses specifically on 55-inch televisions, comparing energy consumption across different screen technologies. This size-specific analysis helps consumers make informed decisions when shopping for large TVs.

Line Chart: Australian Spot Power Prices (1998-2024)

This line chart tracks the average spot power prices in Australia over more than two decades. Understanding price trends helps contextualize the cost savings potential of energy-efficient appliances.
